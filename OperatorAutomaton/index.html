Operator Automaton

A continuous grid where every cell rewrites itself with the arithmetic of its plus-shaped neighbours.

× + − ÷
t=0: each cell gets a Gaussian value (μ0, σ1) clamped to [−2, 2].
each step: every cell samples an operator by the mix above; its op-string is {op} {own value}. A cell then rebuilds its value from its own, applying the four neighbours' op-strings with real precedence — ×/÷ bind first (N→E→S→W), then +/−. So own 0.3 with neighbours +2, +0, ×3, −1 → 0.3×3+2+0−1. Result is kept finite by the bounding mode. keys: space / s / r
